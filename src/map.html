Avila Chevalier will represent New York’s 13th Congressional District, which covers Upper Manhattan and parts of the West Bronx.

Avila Chevalier raised $1.53 million over the course of her campaign—$1.46 million of which came from individual donors, according to Federal Election Commission records. Only about 39 percent of those funds were from in-state donors, Spectator found.

Mapping out individual donors to Avila Chevalier’s campaign reveals support from across the United States.

One of those out-of-state donors was Saikat Chakrabarti, co-founder of Justice Democrats, a PAC that aims to elect a “new generation of diverse, working-class leaders” who can deliver on “our most pressing, progressive priorities,” according to the organization’s website. The PAC boosted Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez’s (D-N.Y.) campaign to success, and Chakrabarti later became her chief of staff.

Chakrabarti, who is based in San Francisco, donated $7,000 directly to Avila Chevalier over the course of her campaign, according to campaign finance records. Justice Democrats, based in Centreville, Virginia, contributed thousands of dollars to the democratic socialist.

Avila Chevalier’s team did not respond to a request for comment on the role of out-of-state support for her campaign or money from super PACs like American Priorities.
